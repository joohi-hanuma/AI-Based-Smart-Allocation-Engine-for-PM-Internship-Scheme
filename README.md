# AI-Based-Smart-Allocation-Engine-for-PM-Internship-Scheme

## Overview
The PM Internship Scheme struggles to efficiently match thousands of applicants with suitable internship opportunities. This project demonstrates a **smart AI system** that matches students with internships based on skills, qualifications, location, interests, and fairness criteria.

## Problem Statement
- Manual internship allocation is slow and inefficient.  
- Students often get placements that don’t match their skills or preferences.  
- Ensuring fairness for rural and under represented students is difficult.

  ## Proposed Solution
- AI-based matchmaking engine to calculate the best-fit internships.  
- Considers student skills, qualifications, location, preferences, and past participation.  
- Applies fairness rules (affirmative action, rural/urban balance).  
- Frontend interface allows students and organizations to enter data and see results.  

## Demo Workflow
1. **Student Data Entry** – Enter skills, qualifications, location, and interests.  
2. **Organization Posting** – Companies post internship details and requirements.  
3. **Matching Engine** – Calculates best-fit percentage for students vs internships.  
4. **Result** – Displays top internship matches based on skills and suitability.

## Technologies Used
- **Backend:** Python, Flask  
- **Frontend:** React.js  
- **Machine Learning:** Scikit-learn (AI Matching Engine)  
- **Data Handling:** JSON/CSV/Google Sheets APIs  
- **Deployment:** Local/cloud prototype

 ## Methodology
1. Requirement gathering (student & company needs)  
2. Data collection and preprocessing  
3. AI Matching Engine for calculating best-fit percentages  
4. Backend-Frontend integration for demonstration  
5. Testing and evaluation of results  

## Potential Impact
- Students get internships that match their skills and interests.  
- Industries receive highly suitable candidates, saving time and effort.  
- Ensures fair and transparent allocation, especially for underrepresented groups.
